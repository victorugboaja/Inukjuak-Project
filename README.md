# Inukjuak-Water Ledger
Water Ledger is an offline-first tool that tracks water deliveries, predicts homes at risk of water shortages, helps dispatchers replan their routes when trucks fail, alerts residents, and creates evidence for reliable water infrastructure for the residents of Inukjuak.

Every delivery is tracked. The app predicts which homes and buildings are at risk of losing water access, and applies the data to delivery instructions, scan for operational constraints, notify households, and produces monthly evidence that drive community project funding.
