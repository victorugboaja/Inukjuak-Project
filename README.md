# Inukjuak-Project
In Inukjuak, there are no underground water pipes. 3 trucks deliver drinking water to about 480 homes' tanks and collect sewage. When a driver is absent or a truck breaks down, many residents lose water access with some cases extending beyond one week without this human necessity .

Every delivery is tracked. The app predicts which homes and buildings are at risk of losing water access, and applies the data to delivery instructions, scan for operational constraints, notify households, and produces monthly evidence that drive community project funding.
